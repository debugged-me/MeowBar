What's inside

Two meters, a little personality, zero setup.

Live limits

Session and weekly windows with reset countdowns, refreshed every 30 seconds — and it self-throttles on rate limits instead of hammering.

Talks like a person

The bar says running, task done, nearing limit or limit reached — then quietly returns to numbers on its own.

Private by design

No sign-in, no analytics, no telemetry. It reads the credentials already on your Mac and only calls the endpoints your CLIs use.

Pace, not just points

Every bar knows where an even burn would be — and tells you in green, orange or red: plenty left, cutting it close, may run out in ~2d. The menu bar can even show time until empty.

History & alerts

Click a provider for its last 7 days and a 24h burn sparkline. Nudges at 75/90/95% and on reset — and right-click the bar for a full menu.

Featherweight

Native Swift, ~100 MB of RAM, 0% CPU when idle. Launches at login and lives entirely in the menu bar — no Dock icon.

See it in action

Quiet percentages when all is well — plain words when it matters.

How it works

Two minutes, once.

01

Download & install

Open the DMG and drag MeowBar into Applications. It's ad-hoc signed, so on first launch right-click → Open.

02

Allow one prompt

macOS asks once to read your Claude credentials — choose Always Allow and it never asks again.

03

Glance up

The menu bar shows each provider's remaining %. Click for details — it starts itself at login from then on.
